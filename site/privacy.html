MyFit Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

MyFit Tracker ("MyFit", "we") is a fitness, nutrition and wellbeing app. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it is stored, and the choices you have. MyFit is a wellness app — it does not diagnose, treat or replace medical advice.

1. Data stored on your phone

Everything you log — workouts, food, water, weight, measurements, sleep, mood, fasting, medicines, blood glucose, blood pressure, cycle data, notes and progress photos — is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not uploaded to our servers.

2. Health Connect

  • Read: with your permission, MyFit reads steps, distance, calories, floors, exercise sessions, heart rate, resting heart rate, HRV, blood oxygen, sleep, weight, body fat, hydration, respiratory rate, body and skin temperature, VO2 max, blood pressure and blood glucose to show dashboards, goals, trends and challenge progress.
  • Write: only if you turn on cycle tracking or blood-glucose tracking, MyFit writes those entries (menstruation, ovulation tests, cervical mucus, basal body temperature, spotting, blood glucose) to Health Connect, and deletes only entries it wrote itself when you edit or remove them.
  • Health Connect data is used only to provide the app's features to you. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models.
  • You can revoke access at any time in Android Settings → Health Connect.

3. Account and friends (optional)

If you sign in with Google to compete with friends, we store in Google Firebase: your display name, username and ID, friend list, and your weekly totals (steps, active minutes, distance, workouts), plus your Arena level, stars, reward titles and finished journeys, and your profile picture: a ready-made avatar, or — only if you pick one — a small 128-pixel copy of your own photo, which only friends can see. If you signed in with Google and haven't chosen another picture, your Google account photo is shown next to your name to friends and on leaderboards you appear on. These are visible only to friends you add and — if you turn on the public leaderboard — to other users. Cycle, mood, medicine, weight and food data are never uploaded.

Blood-sugar family sharing (optional): only if you turn it on, your blood-sugar readings from the last 14 days (value, time and "before breakfast"-style label) are copied to Firebase so the family members who enter your family code can see them and get alerts for lows and very highs. Turning it off deletes the shared copy and removes everyone's access.

4. Online AI (optional)

When you ask Pip a question or scan a meal photo online, the question (with a short summary of only the numbers it needs) or the photo is sent to an AI provider (Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral) through MyFit's proxy, which verifies you are signed in. Voice replies may be generated by ElevenLabs or Microsoft Azure. Providers process requests under their own terms and we do not store your questions. The offline AI model runs entirely on your phone.

5. Ads

MyFit may show optional rewarded ads (Google AdMob) that you choose to watch to unlock extra AI requests. Ads never appear on health, cycle, glucose or mental-health screens, and your health data is never used for ad targeting. Where required, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

6. Phone permissions

  • Location (approximate) — only if you turn on prayer times, to calculate them for your city. It stays on the phone.
  • Camera — only when you use it: to measure your pulse with the flash, scan a barcode or QR code, or photograph a meal. Pulse readings never leave the phone; meal photos are sent to the online AI only if you choose online scanning.
  • Voice typing — the mic button in Pip's chat uses your phone's own speech recogniser (for example Google voice typing); MyFit receives only the text.
  • Notifications, alarms and physical activity — for reminders, live workout and fasting progress, and step counting.

7. Backups

If you create a backup, it is saved as a file you choose, or in your own Google Drive app-data folder. Only you can access it.

8. How long we keep data

  • Data on your phone stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Online friends data (profile, weekly totals, Arena profile, picture) is kept while your account exists and is deleted when you delete your online account or all your data.
  • Shared blood-sugar readings are kept for 14 days at most and are deleted as soon as you turn sharing off.
  • Reports you send about other users are kept for up to 12 months so we can act on them.

9. Safety and reports

You can report or block any friend from their profile (Friends → tap the person → Report or Block). Reports are reviewed and we remove pictures, names or accounts that break the rules (for example offensive images or impersonation). Blocking removes the person from your friends and stops their requests.

10. Your choices and rights

  • See, change or export your data in the app at any time (Backup & restore).
  • Delete everything: Settings → Privacy → Delete all my data (phone and, if signed in, our servers), or delete only the online account in Friends → ⚙ → Delete online account.
  • Withdraw Health Connect, location, camera or notification access in Android settings, and change ad consent in Pip settings.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all local data.

11. Medical disclaimer

MyFit is a wellness app, not a medical device. Readings, estimates, predictions and AI answers can be inaccurate. Always follow your doctor's advice, and in an emergency call your local emergency number.

12. Children

MyFit is not intended for children under 13.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

14. Contact

Questions about privacy or your data, or a request to delete it: email myfitnesstrack.support@gmail.com. We reply within 7 days.
