FAQ

  
Questions, answered.

  

    
Is MyFit free?
Yes. Core tracking is free. Online AI has a generous daily free allowance; you can watch a short ad for more or use the offline brain.

    
Why an APK and not the Play Store?
Store listings are on the way. Until then the APK on this page is the official build, signed by us, and updates itself inside the app.

    
Does the camera record me?
No. Frames are analysed on your phone to find body points for rep counting and form scores, then thrown away. Nothing is saved or uploaded.

    
How accurate is snap-a-meal?
You get a quick estimate in a couple of seconds, then a deeper pass refines dishes and portions using our nutrition database. Values are estimates — you can adjust any item before saving.

    
Are Coach Bolt and Chef Zest real people?
No — they're original cartoon characters powered by MyFit's on-phone logic and AI. Their advice is general guidance, not medical advice. Rename them however you like.

    
I have diabetes or an injury. Is it safe?
Add it to your health profile and MyFit avoids foods and exercises that don't suit you and suggests swaps. It never calculates insulin or medication doses — always follow your doctor.

    
Does it work in Urdu?
The coaches and Pip speak Urdu, and food search understands Roman Urdu (anda, karahi, nihari…). More Urdu screens are coming.

    
Which phones are supported?
Android 8.0 and newer, including tall and narrow screens. Live notifications use the Now Bar on supported Samsung and Pixel phones. A Wear OS companion and widgets are included.
