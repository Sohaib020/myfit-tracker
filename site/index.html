Install in 3 steps

Download the APK

Tap the green button on your Android phone. Your browser may say the file "can be harmful" — that's normal for apps outside the Play Store. Tap Download anyway.

Allow installing

Open the file. If Android asks, allow your browser to "Install unknown apps" (one time only).

Open MyFit & meet Pip

Sign in, set your goals and you're in. Future updates arrive inside the app.
