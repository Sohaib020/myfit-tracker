FAQ

  
Questions, answered.

  

    
Is MyFit free?
Yes. Core tracking is free. Online AI has a generous daily free allowance; you can watch a short ad for more or use the offline brain.

    
Why an APK and not the Play Store?
Store listings are on the way. Until then the APK on this page is the official build, signed by us, and updates itself inside the app.

    
Does the camera record me?
No. Frames are analysed on your phone to find body landmarks for rep counting and form cues, then thrown away. Nothing is saved or uploaded.

    
Are the trainer and nutritionist real people?
No — they're fictional characters powered by MyFit's on-phone logic and AI. Their advice is general guidance, not medical advice. Rename them however you like.

    
I have diabetes / an injury. Is it safe?
Add it to your health profile and MyFit avoids foods and exercises that don't suit you and suggests swaps. It never calculates insulin or medication doses — always follow your doctor.

    
Does it work in Urdu?
Pip and the trainer speak Urdu, and food search understands Roman Urdu (anda, kardi, nehari…). More Urdu screens are coming.

    
Which phones are supported?
Android 8.0 and newer, including tall and narrow screens. A Wear OS companion and widgets are included.
